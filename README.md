### CHEM-000150: Cold-cloud formation or survival of pyrene
- **Subfield:** Astrochemistry
- **Open:** Determine whether pyrene in TMC-1 forms locally at low temperature or survives transport from warmer regions, reproducing abundances and nitrile-isomer ratios with constrained kinetics.
- **Status:** Cyanopyrene detections in 2024–2025 established four-ring aromatic chemistry in a cold cloud. The parent aromatic inventory’s origin remains unresolved.
- **Type:** theory | experimental
- **Why:** It tests how large carbon molecules arise before star formation.
- **Key works:** G. Wenzel et al. (2024), *Detection of interstellar 1-cyanopyrene: A four-ring polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon*, [DOI:10.1126/science.adq6391](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adq6391); G. Wenzel et al. (2025), *Detections of interstellar aromatic nitriles 2-cyanopyrene and 4-cyanopyrene in TMC-1*, [DOI:10.1038/s41550-024-02410-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-024-02410-9).
